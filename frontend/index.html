Who We Are

About TechnoX

Empowering the next generation of tech innovators

TechnoX Society is the premier Computer Science society at COMSATS University Islamabad, Vehari Campus. Founded with a mission to bridge the gap between academic knowledge and industry-ready skills, we create a dynamic ecosystem where students learn, compete, and grow together.

Through workshops, seminars, hackathons, and networking events, TechnoX cultivates a culture of innovation and collaboration. Whether you're a beginner curious about coding or an experienced developer, TechnoX has something for you.

Our Mission
